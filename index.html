About

An economist on the policy frontier.

Monika Schnitzer holds the Chair for Comparative Economics at LMU Munich. Her research investigates how firms innovate, how competition shapes markets, and how multinational activity links national economies.

Beyond academic work, she chairs the German Council of Economic Experts — the independent advisory body to the Federal Government — having joined as council member in 2020 and assumed the chair in 2022. She co-chairs the Franco-German Council of Economic Experts and serves on the Expert Commission for the Modernisation of the Debt Rule.

She studied at Cologne, Bonn and the London School of Economics, and held visiting positions at Stanford, Yale, UC Berkeley and Harvard. She is a member of Leopoldina and the Bavarian Academy of Sciences, and was awarded the Gustav Stolper Award and an honorary doctorate from Kiel University in 2022.
