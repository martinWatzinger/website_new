Annual Report 2025/26 published

12 November 2025

Jahresgutachten 2025/2026

Perspektiven für morgen schaffen — Chancen nicht verspielen

The German Council of Economic Experts (SVR) presented its Annual Report 2025/26 to the Federal Government on 12 November. Alongside the cyclical outlook for the current and coming year, the report covers the additionality of investment in the special infrastructure and climate-neutrality fund (SVIK), strengthening the European single market, the distribution of wealth in Germany, the taxation of inheritances and gifts, and reform options for corporate taxation.

It also includes the chapters on cutting bureaucracy, structural change, and the investment orientation of the SVIK, which were already published in the spring.

About

Economist, advisor, educator.

Monika Schnitzer holds the Chair for Comparative Economics at LMU Munich. Her research focuses on innovation, competition policy, and the behaviour of multinational firms.

She has chaired the German Council of Economic Experts (Sachverständigenrat zur Begutachtung der gesamtwirtschaftlichen Entwicklung) since 2022 and has been a council member since 2020. Since 2023 she co-chairs the Franco-German Council of Economic Experts.
